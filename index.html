01 · About

Who we are

HAZE is an online gaming clan and community from Zimbabwe. We're a crew of players from Harare and beyond who squad up, compete and grow together.

Whether you're grinding ranked or just want people to run games with after work, there's a seat for you here, as long as you bring the right energy.

What we play

  • Fortnite

  • Teamwork

    Comms on, ego off. We win and lose as a squad and we've always got each other's backs.

  • Skill

    We put in the reps, review our plays and push each other to rank up every season.

  • Community

    More than a clan. It's a home for Zimbabwean gamers to link up, chill and support each other.

    05 · Join

    How to join

    1. 01

      Join the Discord

      Hop into our server. That's where everything happens: squads, scrims, events and banter.

      Open Discord
    2. 02

      Post in #applications

      Make a post in the #applications forum channel. Tell us your gamer tag, your rank and a bit about yourself.

    3. 03

      Get the Member role

      The leaders review your application. Once you're accepted, you get the Member role and full access to the clan.

    4. 04

      Pick your HAZE name

      Choose your name with the HAZE tag and the vowel swap. Try the name forge for ideas.
